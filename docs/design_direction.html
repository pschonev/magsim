Proposal for the magsim web app

A board-game look for magsim

The app works, but it looks like a generic admin dashboard. This proposal keeps the layout and the charts and changes the visual language: neutral, calm surfaces for reading data, plus a small set of game-box details taken from your GitHub badge. Chunky outlines, the five track colors, a rounded display face, and a race track that looks like a printed board.

The idea in one sentence

Data areas stay quiet and readable in both themes; the game shows up in three places only: the track, the controls (pill tabs and outlined buttons with a printed offset shadow), and the headings. If everything is colorful, nothing reads, so the five colors carry meaning instead of decoration.

Color

The five track colors from the badge become the game palette. Purple stays the single interactive accent (selected tab, primary button, focus), as you suggested. Neutrals lean slightly green in dark mode to match the existing chart background, and stay a clean paper white in light mode.

The race track

The biggest visual win. Today's track is grey boxes on a dark panel with the Goal tile wedged into the Start at an angle. The proposal draws it like the badge: a black track with a white rim, square-cut spaces with upright numbers, a checkered finish line, colored spaces only where a space does something, chunky racer tokens that fan out when they share a space, and a centre board that shows the roll during the race and the podium at the end.

What changes

  • Upright numbers in the corner of each space; the space's effect (→15, +2, VP) is the main label.
  • Start and finish share the line, with a checkered strip instead of a crooked "G" tile.
  • Warp pairs share one badge color, linked by a curved arrow with an arrowhead.
  • Stacked racers fan out from the space with short leader lines, so six names at the start stay readable.
  • Current racer: a yellow halo and a small pointer, not a thin dashed ring.

What stays

  • The 30-space oval geometry, tooltips on hover, and the step-by-step playback.
  • The track is always drawn as a dark printed board, in both themes, on purpose; the panel around it follows the theme.
  • Racer palettes (main color, ring, outline) from palettes.py.

The event log

The log keeps its colors (they match the notebook), but drops the repeated "INFO" and the debug prefix unless debug logging is on, uses the display face for turn headers, and marks the current step with a purple row instead of a red dashed line that reads like an error.

Decisions for you

Display face: Lilita One or Bricolage Grotesque?Lilita is closer to the badge and more playful; Bricolage is calmer.
Heatmap ends: badge pink and blue, or today's magenta and blue?The badge pair ties the charts to the brand; today's pair has a slightly stronger contrast.
Track in light mode: always a dark board, or a light board?A dark printed board keeps one look for screenshots and sharing; a light board blends with the light theme.
Offset shadows in dark mode?They read as "printed" in light mode; in dark mode they can look heavy. Option: outlines only in dark mode.
